# KDIGO Chronic Kidney Disease Management Guidelines

## Source
Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) CKD Work Group. KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for CKD. Kidney Int. 2024;105(4S).

## CKD Staging by eGFR
- Stage 1: eGFR ≥ 90 (normal or high) with evidence of kidney damage
- Stage 2: eGFR 60–89 (mildly decreased)
- Stage 3a: eGFR 45–59 (mild-to-moderately decreased)
- Stage 3b: eGFR 30–44 (moderately-to-severely decreased)
- Stage 4: eGFR 15–29 (severely decreased)
- Stage 5: eGFR < 15 (kidney failure)

## Monitoring Frequency
- Stage 1–2: annual eGFR and UACR
- Stage 3a: every 6 months
- Stage 3b: every 3–6 months
- Stage 4–5: every 1–3 months
- More frequent if rapidly declining (eGFR drop > 5 mL/min/year)

## Medication Adjustments
- Metformin: safe if eGFR ≥ 30; reduce dose at eGFR 30–45; discontinue if eGFR < 30
- NSAIDs: avoid in CKD stage 3+ (nephrotoxic, worsen renal function)
- ACE inhibitors/ARBs: renoprotective in diabetic and proteinuric CKD; monitor potassium
- SGLT2 inhibitors: renoprotective; initiate if eGFR ≥ 20; continue until dialysis
- Gabapentin/pregabalin: dose adjustment required in CKD; reduce by 50% if eGFR < 30

## Electrolyte Management
- Hyperkalemia risk increases with ACE/ARB/MRA use in CKD
- Monitor potassium at least monthly in stage 4–5
- Metabolic acidosis (low CO2): consider oral bicarbonate supplementation
- Phosphorus management: restrict dietary phosphorus in stage 4–5

## Anemia in CKD
- Check hemoglobin, ferritin, and TSAT in CKD stage 3+
- Iron deficiency common: ferritin < 100 or TSAT < 20%
- Erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESAs) if Hgb < 10 and iron replete
- Target Hgb 10–11.5 g/dL (avoid > 13)

## Blood Pressure in CKD
- Target: < 120 mmHg systolic (SPRINT trial) if tolerated
- ACE inhibitor or ARB first-line, especially if proteinuria
- Avoid dual RAAS blockade (ACE + ARB together)

## Referral to Nephrology
- eGFR < 30 (stage 4+)
- Rapid decline (> 5 mL/min/year)
- Proteinuria (UACR > 300)
- Refractory hypertension or hyperkalemia
