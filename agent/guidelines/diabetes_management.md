# ADA Standards of Care: Type 2 Diabetes Management

## Source
American Diabetes Association. Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2026. Diabetes Care 2026;49(Suppl 1).

## HbA1c Targets
- General adult target: HbA1c < 7.0%
- Older adults or those with comorbidities: HbA1c < 8.0% may be appropriate
- More stringent target (< 6.5%) if achievable without significant hypoglycemia
- HbA1c should be measured at least twice yearly in patients meeting goals, quarterly if not at goal or therapy changed

## Glucose Monitoring
- Fasting plasma glucose target: 80–130 mg/dL
- Postprandial glucose (1–2 hours after meal): < 180 mg/dL
- Consider continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) for patients on insulin

## Pharmacotherapy
- Metformin remains first-line unless contraindicated (eGFR < 30)
- For patients with HbA1c > 1.5% above target, consider early combination therapy
- GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide, liraglutide) recommended for patients with cardiovascular disease or high CV risk
- SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin) recommended for patients with heart failure or CKD
- If HbA1c remains above target on two oral agents, consider injectable therapy (GLP-1 RA preferred over insulin unless hyperglycemia is severe)

## Kidney Monitoring in Diabetes
- Screen for diabetic kidney disease annually: urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) and eGFR
- eGFR > 90: normal kidney function
- eGFR 60–89: mildly decreased (stage 2 CKD)
- eGFR 30–59: moderately decreased (stage 3 CKD) — adjust metformin dose
- eGFR < 30: severely decreased — discontinue metformin
- SGLT2 inhibitors provide renal protection independent of glucose lowering

## Diabetic Retinopathy
- Dilated eye exam at diagnosis of type 2 diabetes, then annually
- More frequent exams if retinopathy is present
- Optimize glycemic and blood pressure control to slow progression

## Diabetic Neuropathy
- Screen for distal symmetric polyneuropathy at diagnosis and annually
- Symptoms include numbness, tingling, burning, or pain in feet and hands
- Pregabalin, duloxetine, or gabapentin are first-line for neuropathic pain
- Foot exams at every visit for patients with neuropathy

## Cardiovascular Risk
- Statin therapy recommended for all diabetic patients aged 40–75
- Blood pressure target: < 130/80 mmHg
- Consider aspirin 75–162 mg/day for secondary prevention
- ACE inhibitor or ARB for patients with albuminuria
