# Anemia: Evaluation and Management in Primary Care

## Source
American Society of Hematology. Clinical Practice Guidelines on Anemia. 2024.

## Definition
- Male: hemoglobin < 13.5 g/dL or hematocrit < 41%
- Female: hemoglobin < 12.0 g/dL or hematocrit < 36%
- Elderly (> 65): hemoglobin < 12.0 g/dL may be used for both sexes

## Initial Workup
- CBC with differential and reticulocyte count
- Iron studies: ferritin, serum iron, TIBC, transferrin saturation (TSAT)
- B12 and folate levels
- Peripheral blood smear if etiology unclear
- BMP (renal function — anemia of CKD)

## Classification by MCV
- Microcytic (MCV < 80): iron deficiency, thalassemia, chronic disease, sideroblastic
- Normocytic (MCV 80–100): chronic disease, CKD, acute blood loss, hemolysis, bone marrow disorder
- Macrocytic (MCV > 100): B12/folate deficiency, alcohol, liver disease, hypothyroidism, myelodysplasia

## Iron Deficiency Anemia
- Most common cause of anemia worldwide
- Ferritin < 30 ng/mL is diagnostic; ferritin 30–100 is indeterminate (may still be iron deficient)
- TSAT < 20% supports iron deficiency
- Oral iron: ferrous sulfate 325 mg daily or every other day (better absorption on alternate days)
- IV iron if oral not tolerated, CKD, or ferritin remains low despite oral therapy
- Recheck CBC and ferritin in 4–8 weeks

## Anemia of Chronic Kidney Disease
- Primarily due to decreased erythropoietin production
- Check hemoglobin, ferritin, and TSAT in CKD stage 3+
- Replete iron first: target ferritin > 100, TSAT > 20%
- ESA (epoetin alfa, darbepoetin) if Hgb < 10 g/dL and iron replete
- Target Hgb 10–11.5 g/dL; avoid exceeding 13 g/dL

## Anemia with Low RBC and Hematocrit
- RBC count correlates with hemoglobin and hematocrit
- Low RBC + low MCV: likely iron deficiency or thalassemia
- Low RBC + normal MCV: chronic disease, CKD, early iron deficiency
- Low RBC + high MCV: B12/folate deficiency

## When to Refer to Hematology
- Unexplained anemia after initial workup
- Suspected hemolysis (elevated LDH, low haptoglobin, elevated indirect bilirubin)
- Pancytopenia (low WBC + RBC + platelets)
- Suspected bone marrow disorder
